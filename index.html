Your learning path

You do not need to understand every number yet.

Use the app in the same order every time. First understand the event, then confirm whether the market noticed, then save it for paper testing. Never start with the score alone.

1
Read what happened

Start with the filing category and plain-language summary.

2
Check the market response

Look at price, day move, liquidity, and whether quote data is available.

3
Inspect the risks

Offerings, wide spreads, low floats, and rumors can change the whole setup.

4
Record, then review

Use paper tests and notes until the signal has a meaningful sample.

Plain-language guide

Four terms to learn first

CatalystNew information

An event that may change how investors value a company, such as earnings, an offering, or a material agreement.

Day moveToday’s price change

The percentage change versus the prior close. It gives context, but does not prove the filing caused the move.

RVOLRelative volume

Trading activity compared with normal. “Pending” means this data source is not connected yet.

Attention rankWhat to inspect first

A research priority score based on source, freshness, price context, volume, and risk. It is not a win probability.
