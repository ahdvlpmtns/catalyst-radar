Plain-language guide

What each state means

Confirmed activityThe rule passed

A fresh filing, a newer quote, and the required current-day price move were all observed. Causation is not proven.

WaitingNot enough evidence

The filing may be interesting, but a quote or the fixed price threshold is still missing.

ExcludedOutside this experiment

The filing is stale or conflicts with the bullish baseline. It does not mean the company is bad.
